# Black Star business blueprint

**Proposed launch baseline · September 12, 2026.** This is an actionable operating plan and researched price proposal. It does not establish the legal entity, activate payment services, or prove customer demand. Start with the [packages](PRICING-AND-PACKAGES.md); use the [operating specification](OPERATING-SPEC.md) for responsibilities and the [delivery templates](SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md) for each job.

## A newcomer should understand us in one sentence

**Black Star builds websites and software, creates content, and helps brands show up consistently. Brandon leads development and systems; Edwin leads media and brand scaling.**

The agency brings one brief, a named lead, coordinated creative/technical delivery, quality checks and a useful handoff. Independent specialists can add defined capacity without creating a confusing chain of account managers.

## Converged offer strategy

The [independent idea arena](../reviews/2026-09-12-backend-business/IDEA-ARENA-INDEPENDENT.md) considers a broader menu. Lead acquisition with **Website Launch** and **Content / Social**. Keep **Development Sprint** visible to qualified buyers, with discovery required when the technical scope is uncertain. Offer care only for sites Brandon can inspect and support.

Do not turn every capability into a public checkout. Six service categories can remain on the website; the billing catalog is a smaller set of bounded engagements underneath them. Brand foundations, automation, launch support and additional creative work use the named catalog extension or a written custom quote. A bundled job has one proposal but separate deliverables, owners, milestone amounts and acceptance evidence.

| Arena choice | Decision | Why | What would change it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest general freelancer | Reject as the positioning | Leaves no clear reason to buy coordination/QA; price-only comparisons ignore scope | A deliberately tiny product with measured repeatability could have a lower price |
| Unlimited design/development subscription | Defer | Two founders cannot honestly promise unlimited simultaneous production | Defined queue, observed throughput and tested capacity economics |
| Website plus recurring care | Select | Clear visible deliverable and bounded follow-on work | Support hours repeatedly exceed the care allowance |
| Content session then social pilot | Select | Creates real proof and tests the approval cycle before recurring commitment | Shoots/travel/edit time make the package margin fail |
| Full custom app at a fixed low entry price | Reject | Integrations, roles and data make unknown scope expensive | Discovery produces an approved backlog and bounded sprint |
| Guaranteed SEO, follower growth or AI revenue | Reject | Results depend on factors outside delivery control | Never turn uncertain outcomes into guarantees; sell measurable work |
| Marketplace with automated freelancer splits | Defer | Adds payment, onboarding and operational complexity before demand | Repeated multi-party payment need that ordinary payables cannot serve |
| Vertical portal / automation kit | Experiment later | Could become reusable after repeated client workflows | At least three comparable paid deliveries and support evidence |

## Competitive promise

Be easier to buy and easier to work with: specific outputs, exact opening payment, one accountable lead, real previews, limited consolidated reviews, an owned handoff, and an optional next step. The [dated competitor comparison](PRICING-EVIDENCE.md) contains lower-priced alternatives. Black Star does not claim to be the cheapest. Its proposed premium must be earned through scope, direct access and delivery evidence.

Rapid development should shorten the build loop while preserving discovery, review, security, QA and handoff. Estimate those phases separately; do not assume AI tools make founder time or rework free. If the client only needs a template installed, offer the smaller suitable scope or refer them to a lower-cost provider.

## Commercial path

1. **Qualify:** goal, current assets, budget, approver, deadline, required systems and service fit. No card charge merely for asking a question.
2. **Scope:** choose a catalog offer or paid discovery. Write quantities, exclusions, two consolidated creative/design rounds unless the offer states otherwise, acceptance tests and ownership.
3. **Approve:** both founders approve their work/capacity, then the client accepts the versioned scope and payment terms.
4. **Book:** Brandon issues the exact project/customer-bound opening invoice. Confirm provider payment, then schedule kickoff once dependencies are ready.
5. **Deliver:** show a working preview/contact sheet, collect versioned feedback and resolve included defects without consuming client revisions.
6. **Close:** acceptance, final reconciliation, files/access, support window and separate case-study permission. Offer the next service only if it solves an identified need.

The existing site still opens an email draft. No received inquiry, payment or portal account is implied by a button click. [Backend roadmap](IMPLEMENTATION-ROADMAP.md) describes how that changes later.

## What we do not do at launch

- Unlimited revisions, unlimited support, guaranteed rankings/revenue/follower counts, or unstated 24/7 availability.
- Enterprise migrations, native app-store releases, regulated/high-risk systems or custody of client funds inside a standard small package.
- Ad spend, stock/media licenses, travel, hosting and AI usage silently included in a build fee.
- Bulk unsolicited outreach, purchased followers, copied portfolios or fabricated testimonials as a growth tactic.
- Unreviewed AI access to client accounts or sensitive data; tools and data use must match the client agreement.
- Legal/accounting services, equity arrangements or employment classification decisions through a website package.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Days 31–60 | Improve repeatable build checklist; reliable intake; portal only if manual process is causing measured friction | Refine edit/publishing workflow and permissioned proof | Three paid deliveries or insufficient sample recorded; review contribution by package |
| Days 61–90 | Offer care only where support is supportable; harden billing visibility | Renew social work only with an effective approval/reporting cycle | Decide retain/reprice/rescope/retire each offer from measured outcomes |

Acquisition priority: existing founder networks and opt-in referrals, useful case studies, relevant service pages, then small measured channel experiments. A proposed first learning target is ten qualified conversations and five written proposals, not a sales forecast. Record why prospects decline before discounting. No outreach is sent by this repository work.

## Weekly operating scorecard

| Measure | Definition | Proposed action threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified-to-proposal rate | Proposals / inquiries meeting budget, approver, scope and timing criteria | Review lost-fit reasons; no target until a baseline exists |
| Proposal conversion | Signed scopes with opening payment / proposals, by cohort | Review after ten comparable proposals; do not infer from one loss |
| Estimated vs actual labor | Tracked delivery + PM + QA + revisions / estimated same work | Above 1.15 on three comparable jobs → re-scope or reprice |
| Economic contribution | Revenue less budgeted/actual labor, direct costs and fees, using the package model | Below package target → examine scope, time and pricing before selling again |
| Cash obligations | Next 30 days of committed costs vs unrestricted cash/cleared receipts | Shortfall → reduce new commitments and resolve financing/collection before booking |
| Rework and acceptance | Included defects, extra rounds and days awaiting approval | Repeated blocked approvals → improve intake and named approver process |
| Retainer capacity | Committed hours plus support against available delivery hours | Above 70% planned delivery capacity → stop selling that period's remaining buffer |

Initial planning capacity: one substantial technical build for Brandon, one substantial shoot/edit block for Edwin, with recurring work explicitly deducted from each person's week. Start from actual available hours after Aether/other commitments, not an assumed full-time agency schedule. Sales, management, admin, support and learning need capacity too. No automatic same-week promise.

## Decisions needed before real billing

Both founders record legal contracting/payee identity, ownership/authority, approved prices, expense responsibility and agreement terms. Brandon configures the actual Stripe account, verified inbox, tax treatment with appropriate advice, invoice branding and test/live separation. Edwin approves creative capacity and each media package's actual edit/shoot allowance. These are launch tasks; no account, signed agreement or legal filing was created here.

Keep client data, tax records, contracts and live credentials in private operational systems. This public repository contains only reusable specs, synthetic fixtures and reference code.
