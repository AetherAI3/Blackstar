# Scope controls and delivery templates

Use these as working templates. Replace every bracket before sending a proposal. They are operational drafts, not a signed contract. Store completed client versions privately.

## Default revision policy to put in each proposal

Proposed starting allowance: **two consolidated creative revision rounds on the selected direction**. A round is one written, consolidated feedback list from the named approver for the current version. State the number of initial concepts separately. New creative directions after approval, new deliverables, reshoots, additional formats, and changed source material require a change estimate unless explicitly included.

For software, separate **two design review rounds** from implementation acceptance. An implementation defect is failure to meet an agreed acceptance criterion; fixing that defect does not consume a design revision. New routes, roles, integrations, workflows, or changed approved behavior are scope changes. The number of screens alone does not describe a software build.

Suggested review window: five business days after a review-ready notice. Missing feedback pauses scheduling and triggers a written date update; silence is not acceptance. Submitted rounds are versioned and immutable. Corrections to Black Star’s failure to follow the approved brief are not charged as client revisions.

### Website scope checklist

Name every route and unique template, viewport expectations, browser coverage, content owner, form destination, CMS collections, redirect requirements, domain/DNS owner, analytics, and search metadata. State whether migration, copywriting, translations, commerce, memberships, accessibility audit, and ongoing SEO are included. “SEO foundations” does not mean unlimited content production or guaranteed rankings.

### Backend / automation scope checklist

Name roles and permissions, entities, authentication, provider endpoints, data volume, trigger frequency, rate limits, timeouts, retries, billing/usage limits, retention, and recovery. Describe failure behavior and human review for consequential actions. A demo that only succeeds on a sample input is not acceptance for production automation. Third-party fees and account setup responsibilities must be explicit.

### Creative scope checklist

Name shoot location/duration, travel, talent/permissions, shot list, equipment constraints, weather contingency, edit count, lengths, aspect ratios, captions, audio/music rights, source/raw file inclusion, export format, review rounds, and reshoot handling. Do not treat a request for a second shoot as a minor edit.

For a website contact form, the criterion is not “button looks good”: validate inputs, complete the authorized delivery check, confirm recipient receipt, and verify failure feedback. For a visual deliverable, capture the approved version and export settings. Treat untested claims as pending.

## Complete delivery package

- Final approved files and stable production URLs, with version/release reference.
- Desktop/mobile visual proof and a short walkthrough of the core client workflow.
- Acceptance record, resolved defects, and explicitly accepted remaining limitations.
- Source repository or agreed source exports, asset/license inventory, and content ownership notes.
- Client account ownership and access verified through invitations; no passwords in a document.
- Hosting/domain/provider inventory, renewal dates, recurring costs, and usage responsibilities.
- Deployment/rollback instructions, backup/restore procedure, and monitoring contact for software.
- Plain-language editing/operating guide plus a recorded or live handoff session if scoped.
- Invoice status, support start/end, escalation path, and what needs a new quote.
- Separate permission for public case-study images, client identity, testimonials, and results.

Suggested defect-support proposal: 14 calendar days after acceptance for reproducible deviations from agreed scope, with the actual window written into the agreement. Maintenance, new features, third-party changes, and additional creative work require their own scope. Do not claim a support SLA until the two-person team can staff it.
