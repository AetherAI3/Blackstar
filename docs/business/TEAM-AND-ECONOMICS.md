# Team, agency premiums and partner economics

**Proposed internal policy · USD · September 12, 2026.** Rates and percentages below are operating hypotheses, not a claim about industry averages or an offer of employment/equity. Pair this with [package pricing](PRICING-AND-PACKAGES.md) and [source evidence](PRICING-EVIDENCE.md).

## Two delivery lanes, one accountable project lead

**Brandon — development and systems.** Aether AI developer; leads rapid website/software delivery, architecture, backend and automation, technical scaling, release, and billing. Parallel development is a delivery method; scope, code review, secrets control and acceptance still have named owners. A generated implementation is reviewed and tested before client delivery.

**Edwin — media and brand scaling.** Leads media management, photography/video production, social content, brand direction, publishing plans and creative quality. Scaling means a repeatable content and brand operation with measured experiments, not a guaranteed audience or sales result.

## Three ways another freelancer can work with Black Star

| Relationship | Proposed starting arrangement | Agency responsibility | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery specialist | Fixed agreed deliverable fee; estimate 50–60% of net service fee as a planning check | Sales, brief, integration, QA, client management and agreed support | Percentage is not entitlement; quote actual labor and complexity |
| Partner supplies client and delivery | Negotiate a fixed fee; 60–65% is a scenario to test only when agency involvement is small | Explicitly priced billing, QA and coordination | Higher payout may fail the margin floor; reprice or make a direct referral |
| Referral only | 5% of first project's collected service revenue, capped at $500; first contract must be signed within 90 days of introduction | Black Star owns scoping and delivery | One named referrer; no lifetime commission, no tax/ad-spend commission, no duplicate referral plus lead bonus |

These are **Black Star proposals**, not sourced market norms. Specialist sourcing fees, introductions and sales commissions must be agreed before the job. A person doing delivery and referring a client may receive both only if the quote expressly budgets both and still clears the floor. No franchise fee or pay-to-join requirement. No automatic ownership grant and no implied right to bind the agency.

Prefer a fixed subcontractor statement of work with objective outputs, price, dependencies, acceptance and payment dates. Do not automatically make their earned fee dependent on whether Black Star collected from the client. The agency retains collection risk unless a reviewed agreement expressly provides otherwise. Pay undisputed accepted milestones on the agreed due date; a proposed baseline is within 14 calendar days of acceptance and valid invoice. Record disputes promptly and specifically.

Before access, verify the working relationship and required agreements, confidentiality, rights to delivered work, tools/licenses and least-privilege project access. Trial work is a paid, scoped assignment. Evaluate output quality, communication, reliability and rework; do not present contractors as founders. The IRS looks at behavioral control, financial control and the relationship as a whole; a contract label alone does not determine status. Federal wage law, state rules and the actual working arrangement also need appropriate review before hiring. [IRS classification guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/independent-contractor-self-employed-or-employee) (checked September 12, 2026).

## What the agency premium pays for

Quote the client's total for the agreed result. Internally separate specialist production, founder direction/QA, project management, direct expenses, fees, contingency and remaining contribution. If the client requests a cost-plus arrangement, disclose the agreed management fee and reimbursables rather than hiding a markup inside pass-through spend.

| Added responsibility | Suggested quoting method |
| --- | --- |
| Brief, client updates, review consolidation | Estimate actual founder hours and load them into project cost |
| Cross-discipline coordination and integration | Named work package with estimate, owner and acceptance |
| Rush work | Optional 25% on affected labor only after capacity approval; never on tax or pass-through costs; no guarantee before scheduling |
| Unfamiliar system / migration | Paid discovery, then a new estimate; no blanket percentage that pretends to remove unknowns |
| Paid media / third-party licenses | Client owns account and pays provider directly where practical; management work quoted separately |

A 30% markup is not a 30% margin. On $1,000 of cost, 30% markup gives $1,300 revenue and a $300/$1,300 = **23.08%** margin. To leave 30% before other costs, the price is $1,000/(1−0.30) = **$1,428.57**. None of that remaining amount is net profit until other expenses are counted.

## Reproducible $4,000 subcontracted example

This is a synthetic custom engagement, not an extra catalog SKU. $4,000 is service revenue after excluding tax, reimbursed pass-through spend and refunds. Estimated card plus Invoicing cost assumes 3.3% + two $0.30 payment transactions. Confirm actual provider fees; use actual fees when reconciling, including any fees on tax/pass-through charges. See the [fee evidence](PRICING-EVIDENCE.md).

| Allocation | Amount | Share of service revenue |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Specialist delivery: 40 hours × $50 | $2,000.00 | 50.000% |
| Founder PM / QA: 8 hours × $60 | $480.00 | 12.000% |
| Direct project expense | $100.00 | 2.500% |
| Payment / invoice fees | $132.60 | 3.315% |
| Referral fee (when applicable) | $200.00 | 5.000% |
| Economic contribution before reserve | $1,087.40 | 27.185% |
| Risk cash reserve allocation | $200.00 | 5.000% |
| Remaining before fixed overhead and income tax | **$887.40** | **22.185%** |

The contribution subtotal is not another allocation; the other rows excluding that subtotal sum to $4,000. Founder labor is valued even if not withdrawn as cash. The reserve is a cash allocation, not necessarily an accounting expense. Remaining contribution is neither founder take-home pay nor distributable profit.

With the specialist fee raised alone to $2,400 (60%), remaining contribution falls to **$487.40 / 12.185%**. The proposed floor is **20% after the stated reserve and delivery costs, before fixed overhead and tax**. That quote fails: reduce agency responsibilities, raise price, lower scope or refer it out. Do not pressure a freelancer to accept an unworkable fee to preserve a percentage.

For a 20% partial refund to the client with costs already incurred and referral not yet paid, the same model retains only **$167.40 / 5.231%**. Already-paid commission is not automatically recovered. Any permitted clawback or offset must be explicit and separately recorded; the agency cannot assume it will recover costs from a collaborator.

The package calculator already includes a 15% labor-estimate contingency. It reports contribution after that contingency. This subcontract example uses actual proposed labor plus a separate 5% cash reserve instead. **Choose one planning treatment and state it; do not treat two reserves as two actual losses or silently double count founder labor.** The 35% package target and 20% partner floor measure different stage-specific budgets and are not competing definitions of net profit.

## Quote floor and cash discipline

For an uncapped referral scenario:

`minimum fee = (delivery labor + founder PM/QA + direct cost + fixed payment fees) / (1 − variable fees − referral share − risk reserve share − target retained share)`

At the inputs above: $2,580.60 / (1 − .033 − .05 − .05 − .20) = **$3,868.97 rounded up**. Then check each payment's actual rounding and any extra provider charges. An opening payment is part of the total and does not itself mean revenue was earned or cash is free to distribute.

Before booking, make a milestone cash forecast. Available unrestricted cash plus expected cleared receipts must cover committed collaborator milestones, direct expenses and the agreed refund/support exposure. A 50% client deposit is not permission to pay out 50% blindly. Track deposits, invoiced amounts, collected amounts, earned work and refunds separately with the chosen accountant's recognition policy.

Weekly, Brandon reconciles provider balances, invoices, refunds, disputes and partner payables. Both founders review cash commitments, capacity and loss-making exceptions. Hold sufficient operating cash for actual obligations; a flat reserve percentage alone is not proof of solvency. Founder profit distributions follow the executed founder/accounting arrangement, not this worksheet.

## Joining and growing the bench

Start with one specialist for a proven bottleneck: editing, design production, frontend implementation, copy or technical QA. Require a paid trial, verifiable work, a scoped handoff and project access removal. The founder retains final review. Add a second assignment only after accepted delivery and measured rework. Do not recruit a large bench before qualified demand and cash exist.

Use ordinary approved contractor invoices/payables first. Stripe Connect is a separate marketplace/platform decision with onboarding and payment-flow obligations; it is not needed just to document freelancer percentages. This repo creates no connected accounts, payouts or payroll.
