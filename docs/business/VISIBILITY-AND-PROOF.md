Status: acquisition and delivery roadmap, not a claim that optimization or rankings have been achieved. Interpret GEO here as generative-engine optimization; genuine geographic relevance is also included below.

## Proof before promotion

Current portfolio entries are founder work and ventures. Do not relabel Aether AI, East Coast Chromes, or Food Trackers as paid Black Star client engagements without supporting records. Preserve existing branding, websites, and founder Instagram links. No LinkedIn links or invented clients, awards, rankings, testimonials, or revenue results.

Every publishable case study should show:

| Section | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Context | Business need, constraints, founder/client relationship |
| Contribution | Exactly what Black Star or the named founder delivered |
| Before and after | Authorized screenshots with date, viewport, and comparable state |
| Demonstration | Short captioned walkthrough or image sequence of the working output |
| Verification | Test/check summary, date, release reference, known limitations |
| Outcome | Measured result with baseline/window/source, or a clearly qualitative delivery result |
| Next step | Link to the relevant service and inquiry action |

Use actual product captures, approved media, and readable captions. Label concept work as concept work. Never make a generated screenshot stand in for working software. Obtain publication permission and redact private data, dashboards, invoices, credentials, and unapproved client identities. Performance changes do not prove sales causation.

## SEO foundations backlog

Google’s core guidance emphasizes useful content, discoverable links, descriptive titles, and readable site structure. Apply those fundamentals before producing large quantities of pages. [Google SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).

| Work item | Proposed implementation | Completion proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canonical site identity | Confirm primary domain; consistent name, logo, description, contact | Metadata and visible facts agree |
| Service routes | Create genuinely useful pages for the six services | Each has audience, deliverables, boundaries, process, proof, CTA |
| Titles and summaries | Unique title/description for each page | Crawl/export shows no accidental duplicates |
| Indexability | Public pages return useful HTML and correct canonical URLs | Fetch and rendered inspection; no accidental noindex |
| Sitemap | List only canonical public URLs | XML validates and all listed pages resolve |
| Internal links | Connect services, related proof, about, and contact | No orphan service pages or broken anchors |
| Structured data | Truthful Organization and appropriate page/breadcrumb data | Validation and agreement with visible text |
| Images / performance | Sized optimized images; lazy-load below fold; retain readable mobile layout | Before/after measurements on documented device/network settings |
| Search ownership | Verify owner access to Search Console | Property verification and sitemap processing checked |

On the current GitHub project site, maintain `/Blackstar/` in absolute canonical and sitemap URLs. A `robots.txt` under `/Blackstar/` does not control the origin’s crawler rules; robots rules are read at the origin root. [Google robots.txt location rules](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/robots-txt/create-robots-txt). Resolve that at an owned domain/host when needed. A sitemap is a discovery aid, not an indexing guarantee. When a custom domain is adopted, define the canonical switch and redirect behavior and verify the old/new URLs rather than assuming a smooth migration.

Do not add client portal pages, preview deployments, private assets, or invoices to public sitemaps. Authentication protects private information; noindex is an additional search control.

## GEO: make expertise clear and supportable

Google says its AI search features use the existing SEO foundations, with no special AI file or schema requirement. Eligibility does not guarantee indexing or inclusion. Treat generative visibility as a measured experiment, not a promised deliverable. [Google guidance on AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

For Black Star, publish concise answers to real buying questions: What is included in a website build? Who provides copy? How many revisions are included? Can you integrate our existing tools? Who owns hosting? What happens after launch? Keep answers aligned with the actual agreed offer and identify Brandon or Edwin as the responsible practitioner.

Support service claims with original case studies, concrete examples, dates, and verifiable links. Make essential information available as text instead of only inside images or a chat widget. Keep brand identity consistent across the site and genuine external profiles. An optional `llms.txt` experiment is lower priority than useful indexed pages and has no promised visibility benefit.

For other answer engines, record a fixed set of relevant queries and dated observations, including whether a citation actually points to Black Star. Separate referral traffic from manually observed mentions. Do not infer business impact from a single generated answer.

## Measurement and release evidence

Suggested monthly scorecard: qualified inquiries, inquiry-to-proposal rate, proposal-to-booking rate, collected project revenue, delivery time, revision overrun, repeat work, and lead source. Define each denominator and period; do not blend submitted drafts with received inquiries.

Public-site event proposal: `service_view`, `project_view`, `guide_open`, `guide_handoff`, `inquiry_draft_open`, and later `inquiry_accepted` once the backend exists. Never transmit name, email, message body, or invoice details in analytics. A draft-open event is not a lead received. Add tracking only with the chosen privacy approach and configuration; none is installed by this spec.

Record crawl/metadata checks, responsive screenshots, page-speed test conditions, form delivery proof, deployment identity, and publication consent for each release. Field performance needs actual traffic; a lab score alone is not a claim about all visitors. Monthly reporting should show what changed, evidence observed, uncertainty, and the next concrete improvement.
