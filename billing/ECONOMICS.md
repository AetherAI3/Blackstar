Fee assumptions: domestic US online card 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction; Invoicing Starter adds 0.4% for one-time paid invoices; Billing adds 0.7% for monthly subscriptions. The two software surcharges are alternatives in this model. ACH is 0.8%, capped at $5 per payment, plus the applicable software charge. Tax, international cards, currency conversion, refunds, disputes, optional products and account-specific pricing are excluded. Actual provider reconciliation remains authoritative. See [fee evidence](../docs/business/PRICING-EVIDENCE.md).

The floor is an internal estimate guard, not permission to discount or send an invoice. Re-estimate if scope, subcontractor cost, hours, provider fees, or tax treatment changes.
