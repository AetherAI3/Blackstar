Marketing & launch support

Bring your next offer together.

Get the page, creative, and content moving toward the same goal, with a manageable launch plan and clear ownership of each piece.

Discuss your project ↗How we work →

Edwin leads campaign creative and content. Brandon leads landing pages, technical integrations, and launch checks. One founder is named as your project lead.

Is this a fit?

For a founder or business preparing a new offer, product announcement, or coordinated refresh across web and social.

What we can deliver

Launch planning

A brief, sequence, deliverable list, and ownership of the agreed launch tasks.

Campaign assets

The specific copy/design/content work included in your approved scope.

Coordinated web and social

A connected set of approved pages and assets ready for the channels you choose.

These are examples. Your proposal lists the exact deliverables, review rounds, cost, and timing.

What’s outside the default scope

  • Advertising spend, ongoing ad buying, PR placements, and influencer partnerships are separate.
  • Sales volume, press coverage, conversion lifts, and launch results are not guaranteed.
  • New channels, added campaigns, and continued support after launch need their own scope.

From the brief to your handoff

The process

We define the offer and audience, agree the asset list, produce and review the work, then check links, forms, and publishing responsibilities before launch.

What you leave with

Approved launch assets, the agreed live pages, a completed release checklist, and a clear next-step or ongoing-support plan.

A few useful answers

Can you help with just one part of a launch?

Yes. A landing page or asset batch can be a focused engagement rather than a full campaign.

Do you buy ads?

Paid campaign management is not included by default. Any channel, access, spend, and management responsibility must be agreed first.

What happens after launch?

We complete the contracted handoff. Reporting, improvements, and recurring support can be scoped as a separate phase.
