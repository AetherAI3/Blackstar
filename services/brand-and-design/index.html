Is this a fit?

For a new business finding its look, or an existing business whose materials no longer feel connected.

What we can deliver

Visual identity

An agreed identity direction, color palette, typography, and logo work where included.

Digital materials

The specific social, web, or presentation assets your project needs.

Brand handoff

Approved exports and concise guidance for using the identity consistently.

These are examples. Your proposal lists the exact deliverables, review rounds, cost, and timing.

What’s outside the default scope

  • Concept counts, export formats, and revision rounds are written into the scope.
  • A new direction after approval, additional applications, and extra concepts require a change estimate.
  • Trademark clearance, naming research, printing, and licensing purchases are not assumed to be included.

From the brief to your handoff

The process

We gather references and business context, develop the agreed concepts, refine one selected direction, and prepare the final asset set.

What you leave with

The approved files, agreed source formats, usage notes, and a record of fonts or other licensed elements.

A few useful answers

Do I need a completely new logo?

No. We can scope a refinement or a set of supporting materials if your existing identity already works.

How many revisions do I get?

Our proposed starting point is two consolidated rounds on the selected direction. Your signed proposal states the actual allowance.

Are editable files included?

We list source files and final exports explicitly so there is no ambiguity at handoff.
