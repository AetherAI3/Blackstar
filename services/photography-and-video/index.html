Is this a fit?

For brands, businesses, and creators who need a defined shoot or a focused set of content for their website, launch, or social channels.

What we can deliver

Photography

A scoped shoot and selected edited images for your agreed use.

Brand and automotive films

A film or sequence shaped around a brief, location, and shot list.

Short-form edits

A defined number of cuts, lengths, captions, and aspect ratios for your channels.

These are examples. Your proposal lists the exact deliverables, review rounds, cost, and timing.

What’s outside the default scope

  • Travel, locations, talent, music licensing, and special equipment are agreed before production.
  • Raw footage, extra formats, and additional edits are included only when listed.
  • A reshoot or changed brief is a new production decision, not automatically an edit round.

From the brief to your handoff

The process

We plan the use, shot list, and logistics, capture the material, review the agreed edits, and deliver the approved exports.

What you leave with

The contracted image/video exports, agreed usage details, and clear filenames. Raw files or project files are included only as agreed.

A few useful answers

Can you travel for a shoot?

Location, availability, travel costs, and scheduling are confirmed during scoping. We do not promise every location or date.

Can the footage be used on multiple platforms?

Yes, when the needed formats and usage are included in the brief. List the channels before we plan the shoot.

What if plans change?

We agree contingencies for scheduling, weather, and reshoots before production so changes can be handled clearly.
